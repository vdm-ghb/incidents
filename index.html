About this database

A developing reference of offshore incidents where weather, sea state, oceanographic conditions or related metocean factors influenced the event or its consequences.

Records were developed with AI assistance, then reviewed against investigation reports, technical publications and other traceable sources. Uncertain or incomplete information is identified rather than presented as established fact.

Entries separate the metocean hazard from the affected operational discipline and focus on practical lessons for planning, design and operations.

Use and limitations

This database is provided for general information, learning and technical discussion. It is a developing reference, not a definitive record and not engineering, operational, safety, legal or regulatory advice. Users must make their own independent assessments and consult qualified professionals before relying on any information for decisions.

Records are compiled from publicly available reports, technical publications, official records and other cited sources. Information may be incomplete, uncertain or subject to correction. Inclusion of an incident, company, vessel, platform or source does not imply endorsement, attribution of fault or confirmation that every statement is established fact.

Third-party links and materials remain subject to their owners' terms and rights. Public accessibility does not necessarily grant permission to reproduce images, documents or other copyrighted material. Rights notes are provided where known; permission should be obtained where required.
