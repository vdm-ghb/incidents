Lessons Learned Database — Oil & Gas Industry
About this database

A developing reference of offshore incidents where weather, sea state, oceanographic conditions or related metocean factors influenced the event or its consequences.

Records were developed with AI assistance, then reviewed against investigation reports, technical publications and other traceable sources. Uncertain or incomplete information is identified rather than presented as established fact.

Entries separate the metocean hazard from the affected operational discipline and focus on practical lessons for planning, design and operations.
