Metocean Related Incidents — Bow-Tie Explorer
Threat. A specific initiating mechanism that can cause the top event. Generic labels such as weather conditions, human error or equipment failure are not threats in this view. Barrier. A control that prevents the top event or limits what follows it. This view shows only candidate failed/absent controls keyword-matched in the record; it does not infer a control was present or effective. Escalation factor. A condition that can defeat or weaken a barrier; procedural, competence and assurance shortcomings are recorded here when the incident evidence links them to a barrier. Consequence. A specific event following the top event, rather than a broad harm category such as injury, asset damage or environmental damage. Method note: terminology adapted for this evidence-linked incident dataset from CGE / Wolters Kluwer, “The Bowtie Method”. Candidate structure — not verified fact. Candidate top event = first recognised loss-of-control phrase in the chronological record narrative; absence of a phrase is reported as undetermined, not inferred. Every relationship is source-unlinked and requires analyst review against a cited source before use as a finding.
